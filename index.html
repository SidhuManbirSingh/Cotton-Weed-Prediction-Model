Maintain real flight duration without unnatural fast-forward distortion. CottonVision calculates dynamic frame timing (Output FPS = Source FPS / Interval) and transcodes output sequences with native AVC1/H.264 codec so field teams can review live detections in standard web browsers.

Technical FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the YOLOv8 architecture, frame skipping, browser playback, and dataset export.

Our custom YOLOv8 model was trained on thousands of annotated high-resolution UAV aerial images captured under diverse sunlight and growth stages. Cotton plants exhibit distinct geometric leaf patterns and structured inter-row spacing, whereas invasive weeds emerge randomly in furrows with irregular canopy margins. The model scores 93.8% mAP@50 for cotton and 76.0% mAP@50 for weeds.
Earlier naive pipelines suffered from video compression issues where skipping frames caused the output video to play back 5x too fast. CottonVision dynamically computes: Output FPS = Source FPS / Frame Interval. If your source drone video is 30 FPS and you choose a 5x skip interval, the output video is re-encoded at exactly 6 FPS. This ensures the output duration matches real-world flight seconds perfectly.
Agricultural workstations and drone field ground stations frequently utilize Intel mobile CPUs and integrated/discrete Intel Arc GPUs rather than power-hungry server GPUs. Intel OpenVINO provides hardware-agnostic acceleration with fast INT8/FP16 quantization, allowing field laptops to achieve smooth frame-rate inference without proprietary NVIDIA drivers.
Standard OpenCV VideoWriter defaults to the mp4v codec and places the container metadata index (the moov atom) at the end of the file. Web browsers strictly require the H.264 (avc1) codec with faststart header placement. CottonVision integrates an automated FFmpeg transcoding step that ensures 100% native HTML5 video playback in Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox.
When you click "Export YOLO Labels (.zip)", CottonVision packages all extracted frames into an images/ directory and paired normalized annotation text files into a labels/ directory. Each line contains: <class_id> <x_center> <y_center> <width> <height>. This structure is directly compatible with Ultralytics YOLOv8 training pipelines.
